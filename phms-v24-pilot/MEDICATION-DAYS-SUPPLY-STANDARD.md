# PHMS Treatment duration and days supply v2.4.29

Treatment type fixed/ongoing/unknown, no default. PRN remains a regimen. Fixed duration accepts days, weeks, calendar months/years; calendar anniversary clamps to valid day in target month then subtracts one day. Manual end calculates calendar days. Separate receipt-based days supply estimate, not current stock or stop date. Sum scheduled dose overrides; weekly schedule reports last complete administration day and next insufficient scheduled day. Interval duration approximate, no calendar end without first time. No date for PRN, conditional, unlinked refills. Matching units or explicit product-specific conversion; conversion bound to drug and units, no inferred drop count. Eye/ear/topical requires total-dose confirmation. Reserve excluded; aggregate and lot totals must agree. Expiry/open-life conflicts suppress estimate. Assumes full first scheduled day, later of start and receipt. No eMAR or elapsed-use deduction. No automatic discontinuation. Real authorized stock, linked refill, FEFO/open life, appointment comparison, reminders and production writes pending.
