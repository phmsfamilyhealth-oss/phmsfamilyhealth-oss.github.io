# PHMS Medication — dosage form and units

v2.4.17: choose dosage form before strength. Strength, administration dose and inventory unit are distinct. Suggested units by dosage form are UX aids only, not a clinically exhaustive or restrictive vocabulary. Custom form and units are supported; use the exact label, do not convert automatically. A change of form requires re-entering quantity and units; existing lot identifiers and dates remain. Combination products, structured numerator/denominator, release modifiers and canonical UCUM/EDQM mapping remain future work. No automatic route or dose inference. Sources: https://www.fda.gov/industry/structured-product-labeling-resources/dosage-forms and https://www.fda.gov/drugs/electronic-drug-registration-and-listing-system-edrls/strength-conversion-drug-listing .
